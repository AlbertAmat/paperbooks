# Printing labels

Every physical copy of a book has a unique barcode **stock code**, which you print onto a label and stick to the book itself. Scanning that label is how the app recognizes a specific copy later — when returning it, moving it, or looking it up.

## The print queue

The printer icon at the bottom of the left-hand menu opens the **print queue**. A badge on the icon shows how many labels are currently waiting to be printed.

## Adding labels to the queue

You can queue a label for printing in a couple of places:

- From a book's [Stocks table](#book-details), click the printer icon on any copy's row to add just that label.
- When adding a new copy, choose **Add and print** to create it and queue its label in one step.

## Printing

Open the print queue and:

- Click **Print** to send every queued label to your printer.
- Click **Clear queue** to remove everything without printing.

Each label includes the book's name and a scannable barcode of its stock code, ready to be scanned again later with the [barcode scanner](#adding-books) built into the app — for example when returning a book or adding it to a customer.
