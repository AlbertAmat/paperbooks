# Locations

Locations represent the physical places where your books live — a shelf, a room, a branch, or anything else that makes sense for your collection.

## Managing locations

The **Locations** page lists every location with its **description** and the **total number of books** currently stored there.

- Click **Add** to create a new location, giving it a name and description.
- Use ✏️ to edit a location, or 🗑️ to delete it (with confirmation).
- Expand a location's row to see the **books stored there**.

## Using locations

Once created, a location becomes available wherever you assign a physical copy to a place:

- Selecting a location while [adding a book by ISBN](#adding-books).
- Choosing a location when [adding or editing a stock copy](#book-details) on a book's page.

This makes it easy to answer "where is this book?" or "what's on shelf B?" at a glance.
