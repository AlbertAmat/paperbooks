# Account settings

Open your profile menu in the top-right corner and choose **Settings** to manage your own account.

## My profile

- Click **Change image** to upload a new profile picture, or **Remove image** to clear it.
- Update your **name**. Your account **code** is shown for reference but can't be changed.

## Language & region

Choose your preferred **language** and **region** for the app's interface.

## Account & security

- Update the **email address** associated with your account.
- Click **Change password** to set a new password.
- Use **Delete account** if you need to permanently remove your account. This can't be undone, so you'll be asked to confirm first.

Remember to click **Save** in the top-right corner after making changes — it stays disabled until you've edited something.
