# Dashboard

The Dashboard is the first thing you see when you open Paper Book. It gives you an at-a-glance summary of your entire collection.

## Overview cards

Across the top you'll find a row of stat cards:

- **Overview** — the total number of books in your catalog, with an arrow showing whether the count has gone up or down compared to last month.
- **Booked books** — how many copies are currently lent out to customers.
- **Categories**, **Customers**, **Locations**, **Authors** — the total number of each, so you can see how well-organized your catalog is at a glance.

## Books added over time

Below the stat cards, a chart plots how many books have been added over time — useful for seeing growth in your collection or spotting a recent cataloging push.

## Recently added books

On the right-hand side, the **Last books** panel lists the most recently added titles, with their cover image and ISBN. Click any of them to jump straight to that book's [detail page](#book-details).

## Returning books from the Dashboard

The **Return books** button in the top-right corner opens the same return dialog available from the Customers page. You can scan or type the code of any copy that's coming back, and Paper Book will mark it as available again. See [Lending & returns](#lending-and-returns) for the full workflow.
