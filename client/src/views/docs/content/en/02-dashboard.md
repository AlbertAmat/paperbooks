# Dashboard

The Dashboard is the first thing you see when you open Paper Book. It gives you an at-a-glance summary of your entire collection.

## This month, and your shelf

At the top, a headline shows how many books you've added this month, with an arrow indicating whether that's up or down compared to last month. Next to it, category pills show how your catalog breaks down by category — starting with **All**, the total count across every book.

To the right, a horizontally scrolling shelf shows the covers of your most recently added books. Click any cover to jump straight to that book's [detail page](#book-details).

## Browse by category

Below the hero, your catalog is broken down into its own shelf per category — each with its name, its book count, and a horizontally scrolling row of covers. Click a shelf's header to open that category in the [Library](#searching-the-library), or click any cover to jump straight to that book.

## Currently on loan

A card lists every copy currently on loan, showing its cover, book, and who has it. If there are more loans than fit in the list, a note tells you so — click **View all** to see the complete list on the [Loans](#lending-and-returns) page. When nothing is out, the card simply says so.

## Books added over time

At the bottom, a chart plots how many books have been added over time — useful for seeing growth in your collection or spotting a recent cataloging push.

## Returning books from the Dashboard

The **Return books** button in the top-right corner opens the same return dialog available from the Customers page. You can scan or type the code of any copy that's coming back, and Paper Book will mark it as available again. See [Lending & returns](#lending-and-returns) for the full workflow.
